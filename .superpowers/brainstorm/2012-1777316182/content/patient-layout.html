Phase 4 — Patient Portal Layout

Which overall layout should authenticated patient pages use? (Home, Profile, History, Booking)

A — Sidebar + Topbar (AppLayout)

Persistent left nav with teal sidebar, top bar with user info. Same shell as the doctor portal. Familiar desktop-first feel, collapses to hamburger on mobile. Consistent with the rest of the app.

B — Top Nav Only

Horizontal navigation bar at the top. Full-width content below. More mobile-friendly, feels lighter. Common in patient-facing health apps (13Sick, GP2U). Works well when patients have fewer navigation items than doctors.

C — Minimal Header + Tab Bar

Clean white header with just logo + avatar. Content sections separated by a horizontal tab bar (Home | Consultations | Profile). Very mobile-native feel. Easiest to understand for patients who may not be tech-savvy.
