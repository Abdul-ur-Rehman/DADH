Chat Panel — Collapse Design Options

Three approaches to collapsing the chat panel. Pick the one that feels most natural.

Design 1 — Slim Strip (current direction, refined)
Collapses to a 28px vertical strip with a rotated "CHAT" label and unread dot. Expand arrow sits at the top. Minimal footprint.

Design 2 — Floating Chat Bubble (FAB)
Chat collapses completely — no strip. A floating action button appears at the bottom-right of the queue area with an unread count badge. Click to expand chat back as an overlay or side panel.

Design 3 — Icon Tab (Top-Anchored Drawer)
Chat collapses to a small icon tab anchored to the top-right edge of the queue panel. Shows two stacked icons: "Clinical" and "Dispatch". Unread dot on the tab. Click any icon to slide the drawer open beside the queue.
